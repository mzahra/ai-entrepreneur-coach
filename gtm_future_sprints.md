# Future Go-To-Market Sprints

AI Entrepreneur Coach (Project 3) is currently working and running locally (Gradio UI, not yet hosted publicly), tested on a handful of real profiles by hand. These are the next 3 sprints proposed to move it toward real, repeat users, none of these are built yet, they are the plan for after Project 3.

## Sprint 1: Public pilot to validate real demand

**Goal:** Find out if people who are not the developer actually use the tool, finish a full run, and find the output valuable enough to act on, before spending time on monetization or partnerships.

**Target user/buyer:** Individuals actively considering starting a side business or changing careers, reached first in existing online communities where these people already gather (career-change subreddits, LinkedIn career-transition groups, local coworking space mailing lists).

**Channel/motion:** Free, no paywall, direct-to-consumer. Organic posts in 2-3 relevant online communities, plus a simple one-page landing site explaining what it does and linking to the hosted app.

**Key deliverable:** The app hosted somewhere publicly reachable (currently it only runs locally), with a short onboarding explanation on first load, and basic usage logging (how many people upload a profile, how many finish and download a report) so the pilot is actually measurable.

**Success metric:** At least 50 completed full runs (profile uploaded through report downloaded) within 30 days of launch, with at least 20% of users downloading or opening the PDF/HTML report (a proxy for the output feeling worth keeping, not just a novelty click).

## Sprint 2: Distribution through career coaches and outplacement services

**Goal:** Reach users faster and with more trust than cold organic posts, by going through people who already work one-on-one with career changers as their job.

**Target user/buyer:** Independent career coaches, university career centers, and HR outplacement services, who could offer this tool to their own clients as part of a session instead of (or alongside) manual brainstorming.

**Channel/motion:** Direct outreach to a short list of independent coaches and university career centers, offering a co-branded or simply-labeled version they can hand to their clients, positioned as a starting point for a coaching conversation, not a replacement for it.

**Key deliverable:** A lightweight partner mode (a shareable link per coach/partner, so usage can be attributed and results can be discussed with the coach's client together) and at least one signed pilot partner actually using it with real clients.

**Success metric:** 1 to 2 active partner organizations, and at least 10 client reports generated through a partner link within the pilot period.

## Sprint 3: Ground budget and time estimates in local, non-US data

**Goal:** The business idea dataset is built from O*NET, a US Department of Labor database, real for skills and personality fit, but the budget (EUR) and time estimates are still an AI guess layered on top, not grounded in real local cost data. Every real profile tested so far during development has been Berlin, Germany based, so this is a genuine, already-observed gap, not a hypothetical one.

**Target user/buyer:** Users outside the US, starting with German-speaking markets, matching who has actually tested the tool so far.

**Channel/motion:** Partner with a local startup-support organization (for example a German Gründerzentrum or Existenzgründungsberatung) both as a source of real local cost data and as a possible distribution channel to people they already advise.

**Key deliverable:** Budget estimates for the business idea dataset spot-checked and recalibrated against real local pricing (at least a sample of ideas), plus a German-language version of the output report.

**Success metric:** Budget ranges validated against real local quotes for at least 10 business ideas in the dataset, and the German report option used in at least 25% of runs from German-speaking users.
